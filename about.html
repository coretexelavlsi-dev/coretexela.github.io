Who We Are

CORETEXELA is a semiconductor-focused engineering company dedicated to innovation, research, practical learning and industry-oriented engineering development.

CORETEXELA aims to bridge the gap between academic education and real-world semiconductor industry requirements through technical excellence and innovation.

Our Vision

To build a global engineering ecosystem where students, researchers and professionals can learn, innovate and contribute to the future of semiconductor technology.

Our Mission

  • Promote industry-oriented engineering education.
  • Support semiconductor research and innovation.
  • Develop future-ready VLSI talent.
  • Build a strong engineering community.
  • Drive practical learning and project development.
